A dead forest cannot sign a peace treaty.

Theme: Climate & Environment

Last updated: 3 October 2026

You cannot negotiate with a desert, once the trees are gone, only silence remains.

Every river, tree, and open green space is borrowed from future generations, demanding our active care and mindful stewardship.

How this connects with our mission:
Social education at Jalte Diye Foundation includes nurturing environmental consciousness so young people grow up valuing clean air, clean water, and green surroundings.

Take Action:
Spend ten minutes today picking up litter in a neighborhood park or conserving water at home.
